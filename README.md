# ♜ Wizard's Chess 3D — Gothic Cathedral Edition

An immersive, fully-interactive 3D Wizard's Chess experience crafted with Three.js, chess.js, Tailwind CSS, and skeuomorphic tactile interface design.

## ✨ Features

- **🏰 Gothic Cathedral Atmosphere**: Atmospheric 3D chamber featuring dramatic lighting, volumetric shadows, stained-glass reflections, and dust motes.
- **♟️ Procedural & Handcrafted 3D Pieces**: Intricately detailed chess pieces rendered in obsidian dark and alabaster marble with realistic material shaders and metallic trim.
- **⚡ Spellbinding Arcane Effects**: Kinetic particle bursts, magical summoning runes, levitation trails, and destruction animations upon capture.
- **🕹️ Skeuomorphic Brass & Slate Controls**: Tactile mechanical plungers, aged brass bezels, carved slate housings, and analog toggles.
- **🧠 Full Chess Rule Engine**: Powered by `chess.js` supporting standard FIDE rules (castling, en passant, pawn promotion, check/checkmate detection, and stalemate).
- **🤖 Built-in AI & Local Play**: Play against an AI opponent or challenge a friend in pass-and-play mode.
- **🎥 Dynamic Cinematic Camera**: Orbit controls, smooth focus transitions on selected pieces, and tactical top-down perspective switching.
- **🔊 Ambient & Spatial Audio**: Immersive acoustic soundscapes with tactile stone clacks, deep resonance hits, and arcane casting whispers.
- **📱 Zero Dependencies / Single-File**: Runs immediately in any modern web browser without build tools or external installations.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (or visit the live demo link above).
2. **Left Click / Tap**: Select a piece to reveal its valid moves on the board.
3. **Move Selection**: Click on an illuminated valid square to cast your move.
4. **Camera Navigation**:
   - **Left Click + Drag**: Rotate camera perspective.
   - **Right Click + Drag**: Pan the camera view.
   - **Scroll Wheel / Pinch**: Zoom in and out.
5. Use the HUD controls to toggle AI difficulty, switch camera views, undo moves, or restart the game.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **[Three.js](https://threejs.org/)** — 3D scene rendering, lighting, materials, and particle systems.
- **[Chess.js](https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js)** — Chess game state, valid move calculations, and rule validation.
- **[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)** — UI layout, typography, and skeuomorphic styles.
- **Web Audio API** — Procedural and spatial audio synthesis.
